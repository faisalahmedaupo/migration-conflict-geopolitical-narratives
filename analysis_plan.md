# Analysis plan

### A. Descriptive
- Number of documents/events by case and month
- Displacement trends by year
- Conflict intensity trends

### B. Narrative
- Frame prevalence
- Frame entropy/diversity
- Cross-case frame differences
- Pre/post-event changes

### C. Actor
- Actor frequency
- Actor co-occurrence network
- Centrality measures

### D. Robustness
- Alternative dictionaries
- Removing high-volume publishers
- Language/source stratification
- Annotator agreement on validation sample

### E. Statistical caution
Use effect sizes and confidence intervals where assumptions are justified. Do not interpret observational associations as causal effects without an appropriate identification strategy.
