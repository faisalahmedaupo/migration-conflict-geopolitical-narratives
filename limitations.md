# Limitations

1. News datasets can overrepresent English-language and highly covered events.
2. Keyword frame detection can miss context, irony, negation, and multilingual meaning.
3. Conflict datasets depend on source reporting and coding methodology.
4. Displacement statistics may be revised after publication.
5. Associations between conflict, displacement, and narratives are not automatically causal.
6. Comparative cases are not statistically representative of all geopolitical crises.
7. Composite indices can hide disagreement among component measures; report components alongside any index.
